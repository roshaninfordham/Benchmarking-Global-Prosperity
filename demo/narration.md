# Demo narration

Times are from the start of the video.

**0:00**  Benchmarking Global Prosperity. Compare countries on food, poverty, health and education, and check every number against its source.

**0:10**  Pick a country. Kenya's profile opens with eighteen indicators from the U.N. System Data Commons, each with its latest value, its year, and its trend since twenty fifteen.

**0:21**  At the top, plain findings. On the left, the country on its own: what improved, what worsened, and where data is missing. On the right, how it compares with the countries or regions you choose.

**0:34**  Add a comparator. A country, a U.N. region, or a custom group you build yourself.

**0:40**  The Baseline view sets every indicator against the median country. There is no overall score, and no league table.

**0:48**  Compare shows the gap in bars, and where the country sits among all countries.

**0:53**  Trend shows change over time, against the twenty fifteen baseline and the S.D.G. target.

**1:00**  The map shows the world as a globe, or as a flat map. Drag the globe, switch to flat, and click any country to select it.

**1:09**  Every value can be checked. This table shows the value in the platform, the official current value, the reporting agency, and a link to the source.

**1:19**  Some official values are thresholds, not measurements. Undernourishment below two point five percent is shown that way, and never used to compute a gap.

**1:29**  We compared all forty-eight thousand observations with the official publisher. Ninety-four percent are identical, and every difference is shown.

**1:39**  It works in the six U.N. languages, including Arabic, in light and dark.

**1:44**  Benchmarking Global Prosperity. From data to insight, evidence and progress.
